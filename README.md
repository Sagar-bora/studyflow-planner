A full-stack student productivity platform powered by AI and real-time collaboration.

[Coming soon after deployment]

## 📌 Project Overview
StudyFlow AI helps students study smarter by combining three powerful features:
- **AI Roadmap Generator** — Upload your exam syllabus PDF and get a personalised day-by-day study plan generated by Google Gemini AI
- **Virtual Study Rooms** — Join real-time study rooms with friends featuring synchronized Pomodoro timers and shared whiteboards powered by Socket.io
- **Performance Analytics** — Track study sessions, streaks, and subject-wise time distribution with visual charts
